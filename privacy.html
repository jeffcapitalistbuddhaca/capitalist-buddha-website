Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Professor Inc. (“we”, “us”) runs this website and the Conscious Business Course. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the free module or create a course account.
  • Your login details, when you create a course account. Logins are handled by Google Firebase.
  • Payment details, when you buy the course. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your messages, when you write to us through the contact form.
  • Free Risk Assessment: your first name, email, overall result, the areas that stood out and, if you choose, which description fits you best. Your individual answers are scored in your browser and are not sent to us or stored.
  • Full assessment: your name, email, birth date, how often you have used psychedelics or plant medicine in the past two years, your answers, your report, and your choice about research use. Some of these answers are sensitive information about your health and life experiences.

Full assessment answers and reports

Your assessment answers and report are stored privately in our secure cloud storage (Cloudflare). Only you, signed in with the email you paid with, and Dr. Overall can open your report. We use your answers to create your report and to prepare your results review. We keep your answers and report for 2 years after you complete the assessment, then delete them; you can ask us to delete them earlier.

If you agree to research use, your name, contact details and location are removed before your responses are used for research, so research results cannot be linked to you. You can withdraw from the research at any time by emailing us. If you do not agree, your answers are used only for your report.

Your results review is booked through Calendly, which receives the name and email you enter there.

How we use it

To give you access to the course, the free module or your assessment report, to email you your free Risk Assessment result, to send you emails about the course (only if you agreed to receive them), to answer your messages, and to process payments and refunds. Our email list is managed by Brevo.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in every email. You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to jeff@capitalistbuddha.ca.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Privacy questions: jeff@capitalistbuddha.ca.
