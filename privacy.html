Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Professor Inc. (“we”, “us”) runs this website and the Conscious Business Course. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the free module or create a course account.
  • Your login details, when you create a course account. Logins are handled by Google Firebase.
  • Payment details, when you buy the course. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your messages, when you write to us through the contact form.

How we use it

To give you access to the course or the free module, to send you emails about the course (only if you agreed to receive them), to answer your messages, and to process payments and refunds. Our email list is managed by Brevo.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in every email. You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to jeff@capitalistbuddha.ca.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Privacy questions: jeff@capitalistbuddha.ca.
