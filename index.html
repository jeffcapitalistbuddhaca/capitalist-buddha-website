Conscious Business Course

This course is designed to help people extract their passion, cultivate a life around it, and start a business where work, life, and sustainable income intersect into a profound feeling of peace. The aim of the course is for students to launch a business when completed.

The intention of this course is to help people who, after a spiritual transformative experience or an awakening, have changed important aspects of their life, such as their career.

Our research shows that 40% of people who experience a peak consciousness expanding experience encounter destabilizing events in their life. Some transition out of careers, relationships, and geographical location. Through this transition, many walk the path of the dark night of the soul and experience years of struggle.

In the course modules, which take about 25 minutes each to complete, we use carefully curated consciousness practices (like meditation and deep breathing), business, and entrepreneurship tools to help individuals develop an idea for a business. We use consciousness practices, like meditation and deep breathing, to help connect with the subconscious. When in a meditative state and, by extension, the parasympathetic nervous system, the subconscious mind comes forward. This is important because the subconscious is responsible for close to 95% of our behaviour. By accessing the subconscious through meditation, you can access vital information that you might not be aware of at the conscious level. This information can help you to better understand your underlying desires, motivations, and preconceptions.

Topics include:

  • What is a conscious business?
  • Psychedelic destabilization
  • The science of consciousness
  • Eastern and western spiritual frameworks
  • Neuroscience
  • Design thinking
  • Problem-solution matching
  • Spiritual awakening
  • Dark night of the soul
  • Capitalism and business ethics

Course Structure:

This is a 16-module course spread over 12 weeks. The course is comprised of the following:

  • 12 live weekly 90-minute sessions that include a lecture, discussion, and Q&A
  • Recorded access to all live sessions
  • 16 individual modules
    • Comprising:
    • videos
    • activities
    • guided meditations
    • graphic novels to cement content
  • 6-month access to all materials from the purchase date

This course is intended for anyone, but we have a particular focus towards people who may have had destabilizing experiences through consciousness practices. To complete this entire course, all you are going to need is: your device (e.g., smartphone, tablet, or PC), a journal, and something to write with.

Conscious Business is a university-level course that is based on several of Dr. Overall's university courses and his academic research.

The Professor

Dr. Jeffrey Overall is a professor of conscious economics, entrepreneur, and author whose work explores the relationship between consciousness expansion, mental health, spiritual awakening, and human behaviour. He has been an academic for over a decade. He was a tenured professor at Ontario Tech University in Canada. Before that, he held professorships at Nipissing University in Canada, Ryerson University in Canada, and Leibniz Universität Hannover in Germany. He taught at the St. Petersburg Polytechinical University in Russia and the University of Bradford School of Management in Britain. He resigned from his tenured position to explore his research, teaching, and service through his own institution.

For nearly 15 years, he has researched mental health in the workplace, including projects with the UK's National Health Service (NHS) examining leadership and staff wellbeing, comparative research on burnout and resilience among nurses in Canada and Mexico, and a three-year, Government of Canada (Ministry of Women and Gender Equality) funded research initiative examining workplace mental health and systemic barriers.

Dr. Overall has studied spiritual frameworks for over 15 years, beginning with his doctoral studies. His research has resulted in over 60 publications, including approximately 40 peer-reviewed journal articles and five papers examining kensho awakenings. He is also the co-author of Capitalist Buddha: Waking Up to Conscious Economics.

Outside of academia, he has over 25 years of experience working directly with entrepreneurs within start-ups, incubators, and SMEs across various sectors. He has taught, mentored, and advised countless entrepreneurs at different stages of building their businesses. This included time within two of Canada's leading university entrepreneurship ecosystems: Toronto Metropolitan University's DMZ and Ontario Tech University's Brilliant Catalyst. Part of his research has examined why entrepreneurial organizations fail and the strategic and organizational conditions that contribute to their success. He founded The Professor Inc., a research and consulting institute that allows Dr. Overall to teach, conduct research, and provide services to the community.

Course Text

Capitalist Buddha: Waking up to Conscious Economics

Capitalist Buddha is the accompanying text for the Conscious Business course. The philosophies and theories that form the foundation of the course are rooted in this text. This text forms the basis of the weekly live sessions.

About the book

If Buddha was a business leader, what kind of organization would he run? Can profitability work in harmony with people and the planet? Can the quest for personal enlightenment transform our economy? In Capitalist Buddha, Dr. Jeffrey Overall explores how expanding human consciousness can transform the way we do business and create a happier, healthier, and more sustainable world. Capitalist Buddha takes readers on an exploratory journey of the human mind and draws parallels between the modern challenges we face and the consequences of the unconscious mind. Capitalist Buddha offers individuals practical mindfulness solutions for building a more sustainable economic infrastructure rooted in conscious economics. Built on the foundation of mindfulness and consciousness, conscious economics is the bridge between consciousness - being mindful of one's thoughts, actions, wellbeing, and economic prosperity. Conscious economics leads to a more sustainable socioeconomic system that balances people, planet, and profit.

Capitalist Buddha is a guidebook that helps people wake up. It provides them with information about their awakening within western industrialized society.
